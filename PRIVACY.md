# Privacy Policy for Facebook Reels Blocker

*Last updated: September 29, 2026*

**Facebook Reels Blocker** is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our extension handles user data.

## 1. No Data Collection
Facebook Reels Blocker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, or user data. 

- We do not run any analytics, tracking scripts, or telemetry.
- We do not transmit any data to external servers or third parties.
- We do not store or inspect your personal Facebook account details or messages.

## 2. Use of Permissions

- **Host Permission (`*://*.facebook.com/*`):** Used solely to run local content scripts on Facebook web pages to detect and hide Reels elements, trays, and navigation links, and to redirect `/reel/` URLs back to your main feed when configured. No webpage content is collected or sent off your device.
- **Storage Permission (`storage`):** Used strictly to store and synchronize your toggle preferences (such as enabling/disabling Reels blocking, URL redirection, and optional filters) using Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync` API.

## 3. Third Parties
We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
Any future updates to this policy will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.
